# Writing Style

## 内容优先级

Paper original wording

>

Faithful condensation

>

Presenter technical explanation

>

Short summary

不要自动把论文正文改写成 3–5 个短 bullet。

连续的专业段落是允许并鼓励的，尤其当它有助于保持论文原始逻辑时。

## 典型文字密度

- Normal academic slide: 200–450 Chinese characters
- Dense introduction / related work: 400–700 Chinese characters
- Figure + explanation: 100–300 Chinese characters
- Experiment-heavy slide: 50–200 Chinese characters may be sufficient if figures/tables carry the evidence.

以上是允许范围，不是硬性字数限制。

## Rules

- Follow the source paper's original logical sequence unless the user explicitly requests restructuring.
- Do not automatically transform the paper into: Problem → Solution → Impact.
- Do not automatically transform the paper into: Pain Point → Innovation → Takeaway.
- Method pages should preserve definitions, algorithms, formulas, parameters, and necessary technical context.
- Introduction pages may contain long paragraphs if appropriate.
- Related work may remain text-dense.
- Experiment pages should preserve conditions, parameters, figure/table numbers, and evidence context.
