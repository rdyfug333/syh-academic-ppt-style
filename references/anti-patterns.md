# Anti-Patterns

Do not generate these by default:

- Colored cards
- Rounded rectangles
- Gradient backgrounds
- Decorative icons
- Large colored numbers
- Timeline graphics
- Business process diagrams
- Three-column benefit cards
- Problem / Solution / Impact cards
- Key Takeaway banners
- Colored section tabs
- Decorative separators
- Visible Sources
- Visible footer
- Page-number decoration
- AI-generated conceptual figures
- Unnecessary decorative illustrations

Do not redraw paper figures merely to make them "modern".

Do not simplify formulas because they look complex.

Do not reduce text simply because generic presentation guidance considers a slide "too dense".

Do not leave half the slide empty merely for aesthetic whitespace.

Whitespace is for structure, not for creating a commercial minimalist appearance.
